# ADR 0005: Pricing tiers (launch scope superseded by ADR 0006)
Date: 2026-11-02 | Status: Accepted

## Target: 50% gross margin minimum, direct sales.

| Tier | MSRP | COGS ceiling | Spec | Est. landed @1k | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Pro | $100 | $50 | MT7981 WiFi 6, 802.3at PoE, gigabit, metal shell | ~$32 | Launch |
| Standard | $50 | $25 | MT7981 or MT7621, USB-C, passive PoE option, molded plastic | ~$18 | Launch (primary) |
| Lite | $20 | $10 | MT7628 single radio, USB-C only | ~$9 @5k | Deferred. Not feasible under 5k units; single radio breaks chaining |

## Notes
- Reseller channel adds 25% to MSRP or margin is gone.
- Controller subscription ($1/room/month) is the second margin line; evaluate after Standard ships.
- Pilot capital: 300 Standard units ~$5.4k + $2k cert + $1.5k first mold.
